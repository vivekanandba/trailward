# Data sources and licences

Trailward is assembled entirely from open data. Nothing here is scraped from a commercial
map, and no source is used outside the terms of its licence. This page exists because most
of those licences require attribution, and because you should be able to check where any
number on this site came from.

## Named places

| Source                                                   | Contributes                                                                               | Licence                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/)                    | Summit names, elevations and alternate names across India                                 | [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)       |
| [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) | Named peaks and hills, trails, forts, temples and protected-area membership               | [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)      |
| [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/)                    | Mountains with coordinates, including peaks documented only in Indian-language Wikipedias | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)   |
| [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/)      | Summit photographs                                                                        | Per image — each photo credits its own licence where it appears |

## What this means for you

The aggregate dataset is offered under the **[ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)**,
the most restrictive of its inputs. If you reuse it, credit the sources above.

Photographs are **not** covered by that licence: each remains under the terms its photographer
chose, shown alongside the image.
